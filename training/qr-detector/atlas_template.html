QR Distortion Atlas

Every scene the corner detector trains on is synthesized: a rendered QR code is placed into a photo with a random warp, lit, degraded like a real capture, and compressed. Each panel below is generated by the actual pipeline code with one effect isolated. Training draws from these ranges endlessly; the held-out set uses disjoint seeds and deliberately harder ranges, noted as hard in each caption.

Stage 2 · Place

Backgrounds and geometry

Codes are warped into COCO photographs or procedural clutter. Rotation is always fully random; perspective comes from per-corner jitter on the destination quad; cylindrical wrap bends the code around a pole or can with cosine shading. Scenes carry 0 to 3 codes.

Photo backgrounds

Random crops from 5,000 COCO photographs.

photo 80% of scenes

Procedural clutter

Gradients, shapes, and text-like rows: cheap variety plus built-in texture negatives.

procedural 20%

Rotation

Uniform 0 to 360 degrees. Corner order stays canonical to the code, so orientation is learned.

always on

Perspective

Per-corner jitter on a convex destination quad, up to steep off-axis views.

jitter 0.22 | hard 0.26 of side

Cylindrical wrap

A true perspective view of a code on a pole or can: the center column is nearest so the horizontal edges bow outward, limbs compress and shade with the curve.

p 0.22 | hard 0.30 · camera at 1.15R to 4R

Cylindrical wrap, again

Vertical axis 80% of the time, horizontal for cans on their side. Ground truth adds edge midpoints that ride the curve, so the scanner can unbend it.

arc to 92% of the visible limb

Small scale

Side length is log-uniform, so tiny codes are common, down to about 1 px per module.

side 26 to 400px | hard 22 to 340px

Letterbox bars

Emulates what the browser does to a non-square upload before inference.

p 0.30 · aspect to 2.0 · gray, black, white

Stage 3 · Light

Lighting and surface damage

Applied in the plane of the code before capture: illumination ramps, specular glare that washes modules to white, hard shadows, and opaque occluders.

Illumination ramp

A smooth brightness gradient across the code plane.

p 0.70 · ±25%

Glare

A specular blob lifting everything toward white, like gloss lamination under a lamp.

p 0.18 | hard 0.24 · strength to 100%

Hard shadow

A soft-edged shadow across part of the code, the phone-over-the-menu case.

p 0.25 | hard 0.30 · to 55% darker

Occlusion

An opaque patch over part of the code. Error correction decodes around it; detection must too.

p 0.14 | hard 0.18 · to 45% of side

Center logo, at the limit

The most common occlusion in the wild: a brand badge dead center. Sized up to and slightly past each ECC level's recovery capacity, and paired with high ECC the way styled generators do it.

p 0.18 | hard 0.25 · area to L 9% M 18% Q 29% H 38%

Center logo, no knockout

The harder variant: the glyph is stamped straight over the modules with no white badge behind it.

knockout 65% · direct 35%

Stage 4 · Capture

Camera and screen artifacts

Whole-image degradation after compositing: focus and motion blur, resolution loss, sensor noise scaled by luminance, screen moire, vignetting, and chromatic aberration. Exposure, gamma, and white balance jitter also run here.

Defocus blur

Gaussian blur standing in for missed focus, the top real-world failure.

p 0.75 · σ to 2.6 | hard 3.4

Motion blur

A directional kernel at a random angle, the moving-camera case.

p 0.22 | hard 0.30 · len to 13 | 17px

Resolution loss

Downscale then upscale, like a cropped screenshot or a distant zoom.

p 0.30 | hard 0.40 · to 0.35x | 0.28x

Sensor noise

Shot noise scaled by luminance, heaviest in the shadows, the dim-room capture.

σ to 0.09 | hard 0.13

Screen moire

A faint rotated pixel lattice, a photo of a monitor showing the code.

p 0.12 | hard 0.16 · period 2.2 to 5px

Vignette + fringing

Corner falloff plus a one-pixel red/blue channel shift from cheap optics.

vignette p 0.30 · chroma p 0.20

Stage 5 · Compress

Encoding artifacts

The file itself fights back: JPEG block artifacts, sometimes recompressed twice, and palette banding with ordered dithering from PNG-8 and GIF exports.

JPEG round trip

Block artifacts and ringing at aggressive quality settings; 30% of the time it is compressed twice.

p 0.55 | hard 0.65 · q to 28 | 20

Palette banding

Per-channel posterization to as few as 3 levels, half the time with a Bayer dither.

p 0.15 | hard 0.20 · 3 to 23 levels

Stage 2b · Hard negatives

Lookalikes that must not fire

Composited through the same warp, lighting, and capture path as real codes, but with no labels: any heatmap response on them is punished by the loss. This is what keeps the detector from firing on barcode texture, other matrix symbologies, and bold logos.
