# QR scanner evaluation

How much better the new scan pipeline reads QR codes than the old one, measured
by `eval-js/run.ts` (synthetic held-out sets) and `eval-js/run-boofcv.ts`
(real photographs). Stages are cumulative and are exactly what the site runs:

- **jsQR**: the old scanner (jsQR on the full image).
- **+zxing**: plus zxing-cpp (WASM) with every robustness flag, the new
  standard pass.
- **+deep**: plus the trained detector (tiled over large photos), bow-corrected
  and cylinder-swept rectification with second-pass refinement, and the crop
  decode cascade (contrast stretch, sharpening, adaptive binarization, margin
  and scale sweeps, off-center cylinder phase sweep, finder-grid resampling).

## Real photographs: BoofCV QR benchmark

536 hand-labeled photos, 1,232 codes, 16 difficulty categories
(https://boofcv.org/index.php?title=Performance:QrCode). No payload ground
truth exists, so a code counts as decoded when a stage returned a payload
positioned on the labeled quad; Reed-Solomon makes false decodes negligible.

| stage | codes decoded | share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jsQR (the old scanner, alone) | 126 / 1232 | 10.2% |
| +zxing (new standard pass) | 689 / 1232 | 55.9% |
| +deep, first shipped build (v3 model, early cascade) | 711 / 1232 | 57.7% |
| +deep, final cascade, v3 model | 1075 / 1232 | 87.3% |
| +deep, final cascade, v4 model | 1088 / 1232 | 88.3% |
| **+deep, final cascade, v5 model (shipped)** | **1096 / 1232** | **89.0%** |

The final pipeline reads 8.7x what the old scanner read, and 1.6x what
zxing-cpp alone reads. Detector recall is 1213/1232 (98.5%) with 76 false
detections across 536 photos (the panel only surfaces high-confidence unread
shapes, and Reed-Solomon means a false detection can never produce a false
payload).

Per category (v4, decoded / labeled): nominal 76/78, rotations 133/133,
monitor 17/17, close 40/40, shadows 20/20, lots 400/420, brightness 81/85,
bright_spots 81/97, high_version 35/37, blurred 46/65, curved 40/60,
glare 33/53, noncompliant 23/26, perspective 30/35, damaged 20/43,
pathological 13/23.

What remains unread is dominated by physical information loss: specular
blowout that erases finder patterns (glare, bright_spots), torn or defaced
codes (damaged), and motion or defocus blur past the module-merging point
(blurred). Cylinder wraps improved through the phase-swept unroll and the
v4 corner precision but keep a tail of tightly wrapped bottles.

## Model versions

| | v3 | v4 | v5 (shipped) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| val detection rate | 86.4% | 84.1% | 87.6% |
| val mean corner error | 4.51 px | 3.19 px | 3.60 px |
| training | 30k steps, offset loss 1x | + offset loss 2.5x, denser supervision | + field-failure synthesis |

v4 traded a sliver of heatmap recall for 29% lower corner error. v5 keeps
that precision while training on the field-failure distribution (off-center
tilted cylinder wraps, etched tone-on-tone plates, hard glare bands, caption
clutter, zoom-blown screenshots), and its validation runs on that harder
distribution too, so its 87.6% is not comparable to the earlier columns'
easier sets; on the fixed BoofCV benchmark it decodes the most codes.

## Synthetic held-out sets

Generated by `make_eval_set.py` with seed spaces disjoint from training and
regenerated alongside the v5 synthesis upgrade (numbers below are v5 on the
current sets and are not comparable to earlier drafts). The `hard` set shifts
every knob past the training ranges; `lookalike` reproduces the field-failure
photos that motivated v5. Many codes in both are unreadable by construction;
treat absolutes as a stress floor and stage-over-stage deltas as the signal.

v5, `liketrain`, 504 codes in 400 images:

| bucket | n | jsQR | +zxing | +deep | det recall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| all | 504 | 5.2% | 13.3% | 23.8% | 84.1% |
| small (side < 48px) | 101 | 0% | 2% | 9.9% | 65.3% |
| large (>= 96px) | 262 | 8.4% | 21.8% | 33.6% | 96.2% |
| cylinder wrapped | 103 | 0% | 0% | 3.9% | 81.6% |
| heavy blur (sigma > 1.6) | 174 | 2.3% | 6.9% | 10.3% | 78.7% |

v5, `lookalike` (field-failure reproductions), 427 codes in 400 images:

| all | 427 | 3% | 7% | 12.2% | 81.7% |

## Notes and caveats

- The synthetic eval shares its generator family with training (the honest
  caveat of any synthetic benchmark); the BoofCV set is fully independent
  real-world data and is the headline number.
- jsQR reads one code per image by design; multi-code images cap its recall.
- The synthetic cylinder bucket wraps up to 92% of the visible limb, far past
  anything on a real shelf; the BoofCV `curved` category (40/60 decoded) is
  the realistic read on wrapped codes.
- Known unaddressed tail: directional deblur for motion-smeared codes.
  Structure stamping now restores glare-blown finder patterns in grid
  rebuilds; what remains in the glare category is data-module erasure past
  the Reed-Solomon budget.
- Field case study (three user photos, kept out of this repo): a v1 label
  curled around a pole edge lost roughly a third of its data modules, past
  any ECC level; the same label as an extreme zoomed screenshot fails the
  same way; an etched metal plate is now fully grid-rebuilt from two finders
  yet its glare band erases more data than ECC can recover. The lookalike
  set exists so these classes stay measured.
